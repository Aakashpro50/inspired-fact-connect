Inspired Fact Connect - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Inspired Fact Connect is a personal automation app operated by Akash Das ("we"). It exists solely to publish Instagram reels to the operator's own Instagram account (@inspiredbyfact) through Meta's Instagram API.

Data we access

The app accesses only the operator's own Instagram professional account data (account ID, username, and media publishing endpoints) via Meta's APIs. No data of any other person is collected, stored, sold, or shared.

Data storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the operator's private GitHub repository and are used only by the operator's own automation workflow. No data is shared with third parties.

Data deletion

There is no user data to delete because the app does not store data about any user other than its operator. To revoke the app's access at any time, remove it from your Meta Business Suite settings (Business Settings > Integrations > Apps) or email the contact below and all tokens will be deleted immediately.
